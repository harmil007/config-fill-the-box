# Privacy Policy for Fill the Box: Logic Puzzle

**Effective Date:** September 3, 2026  
**Last Updated:** September 3, 2026

Welcome to **Fill the Box: Logic Puzzle** ("the App", "Game"), developed and published by **OcenSoft** ("we", "our", or "us"). 

We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting it. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application.

By installing, accessing, or playing **Fill the Box: Logic Puzzle**, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information We Do Not Collect

Fill the Box: Logic Puzzle is designed to be played without requiring an account, email address, phone number, or registration. 

We do **not** directly collect, request, or store any Personally Identifiable Information (PII) such as:
- Full name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Physical postal address
- Financial / payment card details

---

## 2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The Game uses fast and secure local device storage (such as MMKV / local preferences) to save your gameplay progress and personalized settings. This information remains solely on your device and is never uploaded to our private servers.

Locally stored data includes:
- Level completion status and stars earned
- Daily puzzle streaks and challenge stats
- Game preferences (sound effects, background music volume, haptic feedback)
- Notification preferences and local reminder schedules
- Unlocked themes or board customisations

---

## 3. Third-Party Services & Automated Data Collection

To ensure app stability, track bugs, analyze general feature usage, and provide ad-supported gameplay, the App integrates standard third-party Software Development Kits (SDKs). These third-party services may collect certain device and usage data in accordance with their respective privacy policies.

### A. Advertising (Google AdMob)
We display banner, interstitial, and rewarded video ads through **Google AdMob**.
- **Data Collected:** Mobile Advertising Identifiers (e.g., Google AAID, Apple IDFA where permitted), device IP address, coarse/approximate location, app interaction metrics, and device diagnostics.
- **Purpose:** Serving relevant advertisements, frequency capping, ad fraud prevention, and performance reporting.
- **Privacy Policy:** [Google Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy) | [How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

### B. Diagnostics & Analytics (Google Firebase)
We use **Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics** to monitor app health and crash reports.
- **Data Collected:** Anonymous installation identifiers, crash traces, device hardware and operating system versions, and aggregated gameplay analytics (e.g., sessions, level drop-offs).
- **Purpose:** Debugging errors, improving puzzle balance, and optimizing app performance across different devices.
- **Privacy Policy:** [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)

---

## 4. Device Permissions

Depending on your usage of the App's features, we may request certain permissions:

- **Notifications (`POST_NOTIFICATIONS`):** Used strictly to schedule local daily reminders on your device if you choose to enable them in settings. Reminders are generated locally on-device and do not transmit data over the network. You can revoke this permission anytime in your device settings.
- **Internet / Network Access:** Required to fetch ad content from Google AdMob and transmit crash logs to Firebase Crashlytics. The core puzzle gameplay is fully playable offline.
- **Vibration / Haptics:** Used solely to deliver tactile feedback during game interactions.

---

## 5. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Global Standards)

**Fill the Box: Logic Puzzle** does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13 (or under the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction). 

Our third-party ad networks and analytics tools are configured to comply with applicable children's privacy regulations. If you believe that personal data belonging to a child has inadvertently been collected via third-party SDKs, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate corrective measures.

## 6. Your Data Rights (GDPR / CCPA / CPRA / Global)

Depending on your region, you have rights regarding your personal information:

- **Opting Out of Personalized Advertising:**
  - **Android:** Go to *Settings > Google > Ads > Reset or Delete Advertising ID* (or toggle off personalized ads).
  - **iOS:** Go to *Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking* to control app tracking preferences.
- **Deleting Game Data:** You can delete all your stored game progress, stars, and local preferences at any time by clearing the App's storage/cache in your device settings or uninstalling the Game.
- **Right of Access & Inquiries:** You can contact us anytime regarding queries on data handling.
